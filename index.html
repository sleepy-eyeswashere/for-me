A Letter Under the Blue Sky

left here softly for you

♡
23 July

To My Blueberrypie

here with you
under the blue sky together.

maybe on another quiet day, under the trees, far away from everything, we'd speak softly— not about the past, not about the future, just about the present, just about being.

I was there beside you, without expectations, without complaints, just with a little cake, the candles I placed on it, and a gentle smile.

happy birthday, my blueberrypie.

I hope this year will be gentle with you. filled with peaceful mornings, soft skies, beautiful memories, and little moments that make your heart feel at home.

thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every ordinary day that somehow became one of my favourite memories.
from your vanilla
